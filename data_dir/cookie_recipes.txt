COOKIE RECIPE COLLECTION
========================
All recipes yield approximately 24–36 cookies unless noted. Oven temperatures are for conventional ovens.

------------------------------------------------------------
1) CLASSIC CHOCOLATE CHIP COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3/4 cup (150 g) granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup (165 g) packed brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 1/4 cups (280 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp fine salt
- 2 cups (340 g) semisweet chocolate chips

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line 2 baking sheets with parchment.
2. Cream butter and both sugars until fluffy, 2–3 minutes. Beat in eggs one at a time, then vanilla.
3. Whisk flour, baking soda, and salt; mix into wet just until combined.
4. Fold in chocolate chips. Scoop 2 Tbsp mounds (about 30 g) spaced 2 inches apart.
5. Bake 10–12 minutes until edges are golden. Cool 5 minutes on sheet, then transfer.

2) CHEWY BROWN SUGAR OATMEAL COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup (220 g) packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp fine salt
- 3 cups (270 g) old-fashioned rolled oats

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheets.
2. Cream butter and sugars; beat in eggs and vanilla.
3. Whisk flour, baking soda, cinnamon, salt; mix into wet.
4. Stir in oats. Scoop 2 Tbsp portions.
5. Bake 10–12 minutes. Cool before serving.

3) PEANUT BUTTER CRISS-CROSS COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1/2 cup (120 g) creamy peanut butter
- 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar + extra for rolling
- 1/2 cup (110 g) packed brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 1/4 cups (160 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp fine salt

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheets.
2. Cream butter, peanut butter, and sugars; beat in egg and vanilla.
3. Whisk flour, baking soda, salt; mix into wet.
4. Roll dough into 1-inch balls, roll in sugar, place on sheet.
5. Flatten with fork in a criss-cross pattern. Bake 9–11 minutes.

4) DOUBLE CHOCOLATE FUDGE COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled slightly
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup (45 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp fine salt
- 1 1/2 cups (255 g) chocolate chunks or chips

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheets.
2. Whisk butter and sugar; whisk in eggs and vanilla until glossy.
3. Whisk flour, cocoa, baking powder, salt; fold into wet.
4. Fold in chocolate. Scoop 2 Tbsp portions.
5. Bake 9–11 minutes for fudgy centers. Cool on rack.

5) SNICKERDOODLES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 1/2 cups (300 g) granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 3/4 cups (350 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp cream of tartar
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp fine salt
- Coating: 1/4 cup (50 g) sugar + 2 tsp cinnamon

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheets.
2. Cream butter and sugar; beat in eggs.
3. Whisk flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, salt; mix in.
4. Roll 1 1/4-inch balls, coat in cinnamon sugar.
5. Bake 9–11 minutes until set but soft. Cool 5 minutes.

6) LEMON ZEST SHORTBREAD COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 2/3 cup (85 g) powdered sugar
- Zest of 2 lemons
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp fine salt
Optional glaze
- 1 cup (120 g) powdered sugar + 2–3 Tbsp lemon juice

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 325°F (165°C). Line sheets.
2. Beat butter, powdered sugar, and lemon zest until creamy; add vanilla.
3. Mix in flour and salt just until dough forms.
4. Roll to 1/4-inch thick; cut shapes. Chill 10 minutes for sharp edges.
5. Bake 12–15 minutes until barely golden at edges. Glaze when cool.

7) GINGER MOLASSES COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 3/4 cup (170 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar + extra for rolling
- 1 large egg
- 1/4 cup (85 g) molasses
- 2 1/4 cups (280 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp ground ginger
- 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground cloves
- 1/2 tsp fine salt

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Cream butter and sugar; beat in egg and molasses.
3. Whisk dry ingredients; mix into wet.
4. Chill dough 20 minutes if very soft. Roll into balls, roll in sugar.
5. Bake 9–11 minutes until crackly. Cool on rack.

9) CLASSIC SUGAR COOKIES (ROLL-OUT)
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 2 tsp vanilla extract (or 1 tsp vanilla + 1/2 tsp almond)
- 3 cups (375 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp fine salt

Instructions
1. Beat butter and sugar until fluffy; beat in egg and extracts.
2. Whisk flour, baking powder, salt; mix in until dough forms.
3. Chill 1 hour. Roll to 1/4-inch; cut shapes.
4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 8–10 minutes. Cool before icing.

10) ALMOND BISCOTTI (TWICE-BAKED)
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp fine salt
- 3 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp almond extract
- 1 cup (140 g) whole almonds

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a sheet.
2. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, salt.
3. Beat eggs with extracts; stir into dry to form sticky dough; add almonds.
4. Shape into 2 logs (about 10x3 inches). Bake 25–28 minutes.
5. Cool 10 minutes. Slice 1/2-inch pieces; lay cut-side down and bake 10 minutes per side.

12) SALTED CARAMEL STUFFED COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1 batch Classic Chocolate Chip Cookie dough (Recipe #1, without chips optional)
- 24–30 soft caramel candies (or thick caramel squares)
- Flaky salt

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Portion dough into 2 Tbsp balls; flatten each and wrap around a caramel.
3. Seal well; place seam-side down. Sprinkle with flaky salt.
4. Bake 11–13 minutes. Cool 10 minutes before eating (caramel is hot).

14) CHEWY CHOCOLATE CRINKLE COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (113 g) vegetable oil
- 1 cup (200 g) sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup (125 g) flour
- 1/2 cup (45 g) cocoa powder
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup (60 g) powdered sugar for rolling

Instructions
1. Mix oil and sugar. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
2. Stir in flour, cocoa, baking powder, salt. Chill 2 hours.
3. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C).
4. Roll tablespoon portions into balls; coat heavily in powdered sugar.
5. Bake 10–12 minutes until crackly.

15) COCONUT LACE COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients

- 2/3 cup (130 g) sugar
- 2 Tbsp (40 g) light corn syrup or honey
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 cup (65 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp salt

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line sheets with parchment (essential).
2. Melt butter with sugar and syrup; remove from heat; add vanilla.

5. Bake 6–8 minutes until golden. Cool completely before lifting.

18) CINNAMON ROLL COOKIES (SPIRAL SLICE-AND-BAKE)
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
Dough
- 1 cup (226 g) butter, softened
- 1 cup (200 g) sugar
- 1 large egg
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 3 cups (375 g) flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
Filling
- 3 Tbsp butter, softened
- 1/3 cup (70 g) brown sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon

Instructions
1. Beat butter and sugar; add egg and vanilla. Mix in flour and salt.
2. Roll dough into a rectangle between parchment (about 12x10 inches).
3. Spread filling butter; sprinkle brown sugar and cinnamon evenly.
4. Roll tightly into a log; chill 2 hours (or freeze 30 minutes).
5. Slice 1/4-inch rounds. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 9–11 minutes.

20) MINT CHOCOLATE THUMBPRINTS
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (113 g) butter, softened
- 1/2 cup (100 g) sugar
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 tsp vanilla or 1/2 tsp peppermint extract
- 1 1/4 cups (160 g) flour
- 1/4 cup (25 g) cocoa powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- Filling: 1/2 cup chocolate chips + 1 Tbsp cream (or milk)

Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Cream butter and sugar; add yolk and extract.
3. Mix in flour, cocoa, salt.
4. Roll 1-inch balls; place on sheet; press thumbprint.
5. Bake 9–10 minutes; re-press indent if needed.
6. Melt filling; spoon into indents. Set.

23) NO-BAKE PEANUT BUTTER OAT COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) milk (or plant milk)
- 1/2 cup (113 g) butter (or coconut oil)
- 2 cups (400 g) sugar
- 1/4 cup (25 g) cocoa powder
- 1/2 cup (120 g) peanut butter
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 3 cups (270 g) rolled oats
- Pinch of salt

Instructions
1. Line a sheet with parchment.
2. In a saucepan, bring milk, butter, sugar, cocoa to a rolling boil; boil 60 seconds.

4. Drop spoonfuls on parchment. Let set 30–60 minutes.

------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING QUICK NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------
- For thicker cookies: chill dough 30–60 minutes and use slightly less sugar.
- For chewier cookies: increase brown sugar and underbake by 1 minute.
- For crispier cookies: bake 1–2 minutes longer and use more granulated sugar.
- Storage: cool completely; store airtight 3–5 days. Freeze baked cookies up to 2 months.
